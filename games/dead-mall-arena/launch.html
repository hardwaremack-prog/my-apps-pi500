Dead Mall Arena

LAN Edition with Pi 500 graphics. Fight the aliens through the whole two-floor mall, alone or with everyone on your Wi-Fi (the AMD 395 PC can join the same game).

Play or host on this Pi

Double-click Dead Mall Arena on the Desktop (added by Set Up My Apps). It starts the game server and opens the game. Its window shows the address friends type in.

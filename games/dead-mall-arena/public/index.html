Everyone spawns with a different weapon — kill someone and you take theirs. Med-kits, weapon crates, speed, shields and cloaking devices spawn around the mall, and an alien squad patrols the halls. On your home network everyone shares the same mall: same aliens, same pickups, live 20x a second.

Power-Ups

Med-Kit — restores 40 health.
Weapon Crate — swaps you to a random weapon.
Adrenaline — 1.6x move speed for 8 seconds.
Shield Cell — adds 50 shield, absorbs damage before health.
Cloak — turns you nearly invisible and hides you from bots for 8 seconds.
Pizza slice (Starlite Pizza Cafe) — +25 health, or +5 per pizza off the conveyor belt.
Cola / Mountain Dew (Starlite Pizza Cafe) — same speed boost as Adrenaline.

LAN Multiplayer

One computer runs the little server (start-windows.bat on the AMD 395, or start-pi.sh on a Pi). Everyone else on the same Wi-Fi or wired network opens the address it prints, like http://192.168.1.20:8090, in Chrome or Chromium. No internet needed. The first player in becomes the host: their browser runs the alien squad and the power-ups for everybody, and if they leave the next player takes over automatically. Press T to chat. Each player picks their own graphics level, so a Pi 500 and the AMD 395 can play in the same mall.

# Fernlight Frostfall

A survival version of [Fernlight Hollow](https://github.com/hardwaremack-prog/fernlight-hollow), initially designed for an **overclocked Raspberry Pi 500**. It's one HTML file that runs offline in Chromium with no install.

![Title screen](screenshots/intro.jpg)

> It is 4:00 pm. At 2:00 am an icy gale pours down off the peaks. Nothing caught outside survives it, and soon after, the whole forest freezes solid. There is an old log cabin up on the mountainside. Get there and get ready.

## Your goals

1. **Find the cabin** up on the mountainside. The porch lantern glows after dark.
2. **Gather 8 firewood.** Bundles are scattered around the cabin and glint faintly at night.
3. **Light the wood burner.** The matches are on the table. Smoke rises from the chimney once it's going.
4. **Light the 4 candles.**
5. **Eat a hot meal.** Pick one of 18 cans from the pantry shelf (including Spam, Kidney Beans and Mixed Vegetables), open it with the can opener, pour it into the metal bowl, and heat it on the wood burner.
6. **Be inside with the door shut by 2:00 am**, then keep the fire fed until dawn at 6:00 am.

## The cabin

- **Sit down** on the couch, on the edge of the bed, or in the wooden chair at the desk. Press W, A, S or D to stand up.
- **Ham radio:** a solar panel on the roof charges a car battery on the floor beside the desk. Connect the radio's cable to the battery to power it on.
  - Pick a band (80m, 40m, 20m, 2m, AM or shortwave) and turn the knob to tune.
  - You can find emergency nets, an old-timer ham, a news station, a late-night preacher, a post-apocalyptic broadcaster, a numbers station, and a Pentagon advisory about fallout.
  - Transmissions appear as text, and some change after 2:00 am.
- **Survival bookshelf:** a shelf above the radio holds six books on off-grid living: heat, water, the pantry, solar power, radio, and cold-weather first aid. Take one, sit on the couch and press E to read. Turn pages with the arrow keys or A and D.
- **Spare bowl:** a clean backup bowl sits on the same shelf. If the main bowl is dirty and you have no water, swap to the spare and wash the other one later. Once the basin has water, wash the spare and it goes back on the shelf.
- **Water:** behind the cabin there's a hand pump and a bucket. Pump it full and carry it in for washing up.
- **Swimming:** wade into the river and you'll swim once it gets deep. Fish are visible under the surface.
- **Zoom:** use the scroll wheel to zoom in and out. Press Z to reset.

## Hunger and chores

- **Hunger:** the food meter empties over 3 hours. Eat before it runs out, or you start starving.
- **Cooking from a can:** take a can from the shelf and open it with the can opener. Pour it into the metal bowl, then **throw the empty can in the trash**. Heat the bowl on the wood burner and eat.
- **Washing up:** after eating, pick up the dirty bowl and set it on the counter.
  - Go out back and pump the bucket full (three pumps), then pick it up and carry it inside.
  - Pour it into the **wash basin** on the counter. That's enough water for 3 washes.
  - Wash the bowl and it's ready to reuse. Take the empty bucket back to the pump.
- **Hands full?** There's a cutting board on the left end of the counter. Set down raw meat, a can or a book there while you deal with the dirty bowl. You can also wash a bowl at the basin with one hand full.
- **Water for the morning:** the pump freezes solid overnight. Before the storm, pump the bucket full and store it on the **shelf above the wash basin**. In the frozen morning, take it down and pour it into the basin to wash up. The empty bucket can go back up on the shelf too, so your hands are free to cook.

**What's cooking:** every can has an illustrated label showing what's inside, like the Spam block, ravioli pillows, beans, corn or a clam shell. In the bowl, the food looks like itself:
- stew and chowder come out chunky
- Spam is three sliced slabs that brown as they heat
- duck is a scored breast with a drumstick that turns golden
- venison is a steak that goes from red to seared, with grill marks

## Days and levels

- Survive the night to reach the next day and level.
- **Day 2:** the frost has burst the lock on the shed out back. Inside is an **axe**.
- **Hunting:** with the axe you can hunt a deer or a duck. Sneak up slowly, because deer bolt if you run at them. In the morning the ducks are frozen into the lake ice.
- **Cooking meat:** put the meat in the bowl and cook it on the wood burner before eating. It takes longer than canned food.
- The ice thaws by midday, and the gale returns every night at 2:00 am.

## Fishing and the old helicopter

- **Fishing:** a fishing pole leans against the cabin porch. Take it, look out over open water and press E to cast. When the bobber dips, press E fast to reel in a speckled trout, then cook it in the bowl on the wood burner, or press **Q** to eat it raw for half a meal. You can't fish once the river freezes.
- **1:00 pm on day 2:** sunlight flashes off an old helicopter across the valley, and there are wrecked cars at the far end. The compass marks both.
- **Salvage parts:** take a car battery, a jerry can of fuel and a box of spark plugs from the three wrecks.
- **Fit the parts** to the helicopter, then start the engine. When the rotor spools up you're flying: W forward, S back, A and D to turn, Space to climb, and C (or Q or Ctrl) to descend. Get low over dry ground and press E to land. There's fuel for a short flight. Hollis kept a **spare jerry can** tucked in a corner of the shed behind the cabin (it opens on day 2). Carry it to the helicopter and pour it in for more flying time.

## Saving

- The game saves itself every 20 seconds and whenever you leave the page. **Continue** on the title screen picks up where you left off.
- If you die, you can **load your last save** or start again.
- Changing the detail level with the buttons saves your game and resumes it after the reload. The 1 to 4 keys don't change detail during a game, so you can't reset it by accident.
- Cooking runs on game time, so Fast (T) speeds up cooking along with everything else.

## Danger

- Bundles of firewood are scattered through **the Blackwood** to tempt you in.
- When the Rootwalker spots you, you get a warning: **"It has seen you · run"**. Hold Shift to run.

## The radio shack

- **Antenna:** a lattice antenna tower with a beam antenna stands beside the cabin, with coax running into the radio.
- **Battery:** a meter on the wall shows the radio battery as a bar and a percentage, with a green light that glows while the roof panel is charging it. A full charge gives about **8 hours** of radio time, and the roof panel recharges it in daylight.
- **Special stations:**
  - **15.000 MHz:** sounds like the WWV time station, with second ticks, minute tones, alternating 500/600 Hz tones and a voice announcing UTC.
  - **7.030 MHz:** Morse code you can hear, decoded on screen as it's sent.
  - **14.070 MHz:** PSK31 digital mode. You hear the warble and watch decoded keyboard-to-keyboard traffic from Australia, South America and Antarctic research stations, who are wondering why the north went quiet.
  - **9.395 MHz:** a numbers station read out loud in an eerie voice where the system has one, or with tones where it doesn't.
- **Once the world is fully frozen:** tuning gets much tighter, and a creepy organ rendition of Bach's *Toccata and Fugue in D minor* plays.

## Day 3: the air raid

> Warning: spoilers.

- **8:00 am on day 3**, after the second night, an air raid siren starts wailing from the top of the nearby mountain.
- **Get on the ham radio** and check the **2m band**. Five stations are on the air: the county emergency repeater (145.110), State Patrol dispatch (146.940), the valley emergency net (147.330), the dam crew (146.520) and Old Hollis (145.490). They all say the same thing: ICBMs from Russia are inbound and you need to take cover.
- **Look behind the shed.** A steel box is bolted to the back wall. Open it and press the red button, and a huge blast door grinds out of the mountain behind you.
- **Inside the bunker** a single bulb hangs from the ceiling over a couch, and a TV wired to a car battery shows a live feed from the camera on top of your antenna tower.
- **9:30 am:** the siren sounds again. **9:33 am:** ground zero. On the TV, the feed whites out and the sensor blooms as the fireball turns into a rolling, orange-lit mushroom cloud with condensation rings and a dust surge. The shockwave knocks out the signal, and when the picture comes back it's torn, grainy and shaking, with fires burning across the flattened valley.
- About ten minutes later the feed dies, the screen goes dark, and it's **game over**. The game restarts on its own.
- If you're still outside at 9:33, you won't make it.
- **The story:** after the ending, **Read the story** explains everything: who lit the bonfire you saw when you arrived, who the voices on the radio were, where the helicopter and wrecked cars came from, and why the missiles were launched. Once you've reached the ending, it's also on the title screen.

## The storm

- **From 1:30 am** a blizzard tears off the mountaintops.
- **At 2:00 am** the gale arrives. Outside you have only seconds. Leaving the cabin door open lets the cold in.
- **Soon after**, the forest crystallizes into ice from the peaks down: trees, grass, rocks, the cabin and then the river.
- **Inside the cabin**, frost creeps across the windows and the door.
- **3:30 am and 4:30 am:** something knocks on the cabin door, three slow knocks and then three more. It's loudest inside, and the cabin shakes a little. Don't open it.
- **If the wood burner goes out** during the storm, the cold finds you. Each log burns about an hour.
- **Make it to dawn** and you can walk out into a valley frozen in glass.

## Run it

1. Copy `index.html` to the Pi.
2. Open it in Chromium.
3. Turn on **Sound** for the wind, the fire and the storm.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Mouse / click | Look around. Click once to lock the mouse; Esc releases it. |
| W A S D | Walk |
| Shift | Sprint (uses stamina) |
| Space | Jump |
| E or click | Use, pick up, light |
| L | Lantern on / off |
| Scroll wheel | Zoom in / out (Z resets) |
| Stove button | Turn the wood burner crackle on or off |
| Radio | Drag or scroll the knob, arrow keys tune and change band, Shift for big steps, Esc closes |
| P or the Pause button | Pause and resume. Time, hunger, the fire, the Rootwalker and sound all stop, and the game saves. Esc also resumes, and switching away from the window pauses automatically. |
| X or the Tool button | Switch what's in your hand: bare hands, the axe (for hunting) or the fishing pole (for fishing) |
| Q | Eat a raw trout you're holding (half as filling) |
| T | Speed up time |
| 1 2 3 4 | Low, medium, Pi5 OC, high detail (title screen only; use the buttons during a game) |
| H | Hide the overlay |
| F | Fullscreen |

**Free roam** on the title screen gives you the original flying explorer with no storm.

## Initially designed for an overclocked RPi 500

- When it detects the Pi's VideoCore GPU, it picks the **Pi5 OC** preset. That means more trees, grass, flowers and snow than Medium.
- Render resolution still adapts on its own to hold a smooth frame rate.
- Ice, frost, snow and smoke are done in lightweight shaders and particles, and the cabin's candle and stove light is calculated only on the cabin itself.

Built with [three.js](https://threejs.org) r128, which is bundled into the file.
